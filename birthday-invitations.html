What is a digital birthday invitation?

A digital birthday invitation is an online invitation you send by text, email or messaging app. Instead of handing out paper cards, you share one link that opens a colorful page with the date, time, address and an RSVP button. Every MyInviteQR invitation also comes with a QR code you can print for signs or party bags.

Parents like digital invitations because they can be finished in minutes, even the night before a party. You can change the address or the start time later and everyone sees the update, and you get an accurate headcount for cake, food and favors without chasing anyone in group chats.

Tips for a birthday invitation people answer

  • Include the start and end time so parents know when to pick up.
  • Add the address and parking notes directly in the invitation.
  • Ask for a reply at least a week before the party.
  • Mention allergies or themes (costumes, swimwear) in the details.
  • Share the link in the class or family group chat so everyone gets it at once.

Birthday invitation questions

How do I send a digital birthday invitation?

Publish your invitation and share the link by text, WhatsApp or email. You can also print the QR code and add it to a party sign.

Can I make a kids birthday invitation?

Yes. Choose a playful template like Balloon Bash or Candy Party, add a photo and your party details.

Do parents need to install anything to RSVP?

No. Guests open the link in their phone browser and reply in seconds.

What if I need to change the time or place?

Edit your invitation and every guest sees the update at the same link.

Can I send the invitation by text message or WhatsApp?

Yes. Copy your invitation link and paste it into any text or chat app. Guests tap the link to open the invitation.

Can I use it for adult birthdays and milestone parties?

Yes. Choose an elegant or modern template for 30th, 40th, 50th and other milestone birthdays.

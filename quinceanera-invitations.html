What is a digital quinceañera invitation?

A digital quinceañera invitation is an online invitation for a fifteenth birthday celebration, shared as a link or QR code. It can include the Mass, the reception, the schedule, the dress code and an RSVP button, in English, Spanish or both.

Quinceañeras often have hundreds of guests and many moving parts, so a single page that families can open on their phones makes a big difference. Edit details after sending, group guests by family and track every reply in one list.

Tips for quinceañera invitations

  • Include both the ceremony and the reception with times and addresses.
  • Write the invitation in the language your family reads most, or add both.
  • Use groups for family, friends and the court of honor.
  • Ask for a reply at least a month ahead for a large celebration.
  • Add a QR code to programs and welcome signs.

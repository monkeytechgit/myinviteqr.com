What is a digital baby shower invitation?

A digital baby shower invitation is an online invitation with the date, place and details of the shower, sent as a link or QR code. Guests open it on their phone and reply in seconds, so you do not have to collect answers by text or mail.

Because it is online, you can add a registry note, parking information or a co-host contact, and update it whenever plans change. It also works for gender reveals, sip-and-see events and couples showers.

Tips for baby shower invitations

  • Send invitations 4 to 6 weeks before the shower.
  • Mention where the registry is, or add a gift note on the invitation.
  • Set the reply-by date about a week before so you can plan food.
  • Choose soft neutral colors if you are keeping the gender a surprise.
  • Share the link with the host committee so everyone invites from the same source.

Baby shower invitation questions

Can I use MyInviteQR for a gender reveal invitation?

Yes. Choose a template you like, edit the text and share it. Many hosts use the Starry Night or Little Star designs.

How far ahead should I send baby shower invitations?

Most hosts send them 4 to 6 weeks before the shower, with a reply-by date about a week before the event.

Can I add a registry link?

Yes. Add text or a link anywhere on your invitation using the editor.

Is there a limit on guests?

Essential supports up to 50 guests and Premium up to 1,000.

Can I ask guests to bring a book or a diaper pack?

Yes. Add a short line in the details of your invitation, for example: instead of a card, bring a favorite children's book.

Can I make a co-ed baby shower invitation?

Yes. Choose any template and edit the text. Many hosts use modern or botanical designs for couples showers.
